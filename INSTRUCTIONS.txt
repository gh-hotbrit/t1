Click the Settings tab located near the top right of your repository's navigation bar.
Look at the left-hand sidebar menu, scroll to the "Code and automation" section, and click Pages.
Under the Build and deployment section, look for the Source dropdown menu.
Ensure it is set to Deploy from a branch.
Under Branch, change the dropdown selection from None to main (or master), leave the folder as /root, and click Save.

Visit Your Live Site
=====================
Wait roughly 1 to 2 minutes for GitHub to process and build the files in the background.
Refresh the Pages settings screen.
You will see a live notification at the top of the section stating: "Your site is live at..." accompanied by your custom URL.


likely https://<username>.github.io/<repo>/
